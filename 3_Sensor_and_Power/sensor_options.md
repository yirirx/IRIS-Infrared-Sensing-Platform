# Sensor Options — Evaluation Pending

No physical sensor has been selected. Selection depends on modulation frequency, wavelength, field of view, ambient-light tolerance, supply voltage, and whether raw waveform samples are required.

| Option | Potential advantage | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| Integrated demodulating IR receiver | Simple digital output | Carrier bands, AGC behaviour, ambient-light limits |
| Photodiode + analogue front end | Flexible access to waveform | Bias, noise, bandwidth, gain, ADC needs |
| Digital light sensor | Convenient interface | Does it support required modulation bandwidth? |

Do not order components until the experiment requirements and datasheets have been reviewed.
