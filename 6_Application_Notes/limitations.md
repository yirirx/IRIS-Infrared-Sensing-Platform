# Model Limitations and Claim Policy

## Included
- Deterministic on/off-keyed sinusoidal reference burst.
- Unit-variance additive white Gaussian noise.
- Energy and matched-filter detector scores.
- Noise-only empirical threshold calibration and independent false-alarm validation.
- Monte Carlo detection probabilities, Wilson confidence intervals, and empirical ROC coordinates.

## Not included
- Optical power budget, distance, lens/aperture, beam divergence, or reflection geometry.
- Emitter spectrum, photodiode responsivity, shot noise, or dark current.
- Transimpedance-amplifier bandwidth, noise, stability, or saturation.
- ADC quantization, sampling jitter, or timing/frequency mismatch.
- Ambient sunlight, fluorescent/LED flicker, temperature drift, or component tolerances.
- Actual sensor modules, embedded firmware, or physical measurements.

## Claim policy
Do not claim detection distance, calibrated sensitivity, medical usefulness, or hardware reliability from this simulation. Such claims require a defined physical setup, actual parts, repeatable measurements, and documented uncertainty. The confidence intervals here quantify Monte Carlo sampling uncertainty only.
