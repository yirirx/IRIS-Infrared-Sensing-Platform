# SIM-002 — Independent Threshold Calibration and ROC Analysis

## Research question
At a fixed target false-alarm probability, how does detection probability change with SNR for energy detection versus matched-filter detection of a known burst?

## Signal and channel model
- Reference: deterministic on/off-keyed sinusoidal burst generated by `make_burst()`.
- Noise: zero-mean, unit-variance additive white Gaussian noise (AWGN).
- SNR: `10 log10(mean(clean_signal**2) / noise_variance)`, averaged over the complete observation window.
- Clean signal power changes per SNR; noise variance remains fixed at 1.
- This is an abstract numerical model, not a calibrated optical or hardware model.

## Procedure
1. Generate noise-only threshold-calibration records.
2. Set separate detector thresholds from the requested upper quantile.
3. Validate on an independent noise-only dataset.
4. Estimate signal-present detection probability across SNR.
5. Report Wilson 95% confidence intervals.
6. At the selected ROC SNR, sweep detector-score thresholds and export empirical ROC coordinates.
7. Export CSV metrics and plots with provenance labels.

## Baseline
- Seed: `20261014`
- Signal-present trials per SNR: 1,000
- Calibration records: 5,000
- Independent validation records: 5,000
- Target false-alarm probability: 0.05
- SNR sweep: -36 to 0 dB in 3 dB steps
- ROC SNR: -24 dB

## Interpretation limits
Finite-sample false-alarm rates will not exactly equal the target. Confidence intervals describe Monte Carlo uncertainty under the stated model, not uncertainty in real optics. The matched filter assumes knowledge of the reference; timing and frequency mismatch are not yet modeled. Do not infer physical detection range or hardware performance.
